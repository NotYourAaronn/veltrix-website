01 / THE IDEA

Not another
reskin.

Veltrix is an independent Linux project being assembled from the Linux From Scratch foundation. The goal: understand every layer, shape the experience deliberately, and build something that feels like its own world.

This is an active work in progress. The design previews on this site are concepts, not finished system features.
